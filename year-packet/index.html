Year Packet

Taxfix already stores the PDF. Year Packet checks whether this year still needs something from you — a home-office day log and a payment-year check you cannot reconstruct in March — in a tone that is calm on purpose.

  • Day Meter — home-office days × €6 (cap 210 / €1,260) against the €1,230 allowance the tax office applies automatically. Receipt euros never feed it.
  • One question per “maybe” — “Was this mostly for work?” turns an unclear receipt into keep or noise, with the reason. Resolved questions are the only thing we count.
  • Completeness, not storage — craftsman: labour share + bank transfer; donation over €300: fetch the Zuwendungsbestätigung now.
  • Claim Guard — no refund or deduction euro in any sentence, ever.
  • Handoff — each kept item already answers a Taxfix filing question.

Built in Cursor · adversarial review by Grok · deterministic rules over demo receipts · CLI twin ./demo.sh
