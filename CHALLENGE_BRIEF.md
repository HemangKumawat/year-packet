## Name
**Tax 365** — from deadline day to year-round. Make Taxes Year Around.

## Build (verbatim)
The feature, flow or experience that creates **voluntary, recurring engagement with Taxfix outside of filing season**.

Not reminders. Not nudges. Someone opens the app in **November because they want to**. Return loop = **next month**, not next July.

## Binding for the demo
1. **Not** engagement for its own sake. No feature theatre, no notification theatre, no streaks, no manufactured deadlines.
2. **Tone is what they score.** Users must *not* get the same stressed tax tone. In control, financially savvy, a little bit clever — not dutiful, anxious, or guilty. Design the emotional outcome. → every phone string audited for tension words (beat / pressure / locked / veto / urgency).
3. Inspiration offered by Taxfix: "how can you help people stay on taxes **every week**?" → gentle weekly rhythm is welcome, nagging is not.
4. Stay close to Taxfix scope (closeness is a scored criterion); other models/credits allowed. Filing-only features fail.
5. Jury: 25% innovation · 25% Cursor multi-agent (agents/MCP/SDK/CLI) · 25% Taxfix scope fit (off-season, especially Q4) · 25% demo quality (working prototype, 2–3 min).
6. Deliverable: **screen recording without sound**, ≤2 min, uploaded to the folder by **21:00**. Upload from venue laptop.
7. Prizes need ≥4 members. Hemang is solo → compete, no prize. (`_README` cash €; slides Cursor-credit $. Ignore.)
8. Don't need to be beautiful.
